# YouTube Strategy Skill

The following five workflows come from the user's reference screenshots and are converted into reusable agent tasks.

## 1. Niche & format finder
Find 10 underserved faceless long-form niches. For each return: core audience, why viewers would watch a full video, 3 title ideas, and the closest existing channel if any.

## 2. Topic/title research
For a selected niche, generate 20 topic ideas. Each must include a curiosity-driven title under 60 characters, the core promise, and why a viewer may choose it over a similar video.

## 3. Retention-first script structure
Write a cold-open hook for the first 15 seconds, a specific curiosity gap, 3–5 escalating story beats or reveals, mini-cliffhangers/re-hooks, and a payoff that closes the loop.

## 4. Script performance breakdown
Given a transcript or script, identify the hook, re-hooks, pattern interrupts, narrative tension, specificity, pacing risks, and how the ending can drive subscriptions or the next-video click.

## 5. Monetization/offer ideas
Generate 10 realistic revenue paths beyond AdSense, including sponsorship angles, products, digital offers and recurring formats. Explain why each fits the specific audience.

## Evidence rule
Do not invent channel performance, search volume or competitor data. When real-world evidence is requested, research it and clearly separate verified facts from estimates and hypotheses.

## Agent chain
niche -> topics/titles -> script -> retention review -> monetization -> visual assets -> final review
